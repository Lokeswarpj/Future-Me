Founder Lab AI Hack

Elevate your prompting skills with these frameworks.

Hack 01

Identity Prompting

Don’t ask AI what to do. Ask it to respond as the future version of you who has already solved the specific problem you are facing.

Hack 02

Constraint Prompting

Add strict limits like time, budget, energy, and current skill level to the prompt to force the AI to give highly practical, actionable answers.

Hack 03

Reverse Fear Prompting

Instead of asking for a plan, ask the AI what specific mistake your future self would warn you about today regarding your current goal.
